# Final narration

New medicines need rigorous clinical trials. But recruiting participants and reviewing changing records can delay progress.

ProtocolPulse connects that work. AI turns a trial protocol into source-linked rules, reviewed and approved by a clinical lead.

Screening checks patient records against those rules, showing potential matches, conflicts, and missing evidence. Teams can focus their review where it matters.

After participation begins, oversight continues. A new medication record triggers a targeted check. A possible protocol conflict becomes a prioritized case, with evidence ready for review.

The clinical lead decides what happens next.

By streamlining trial operations, ProtocolPulse could help promising medicines move closer to patients. AI assists. People decide.
