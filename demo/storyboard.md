# Keep the trial moving

New production: a continuous, cursor-led real application journey framed by a bespoke motion identity. Exactly sixty seconds; no reused video frames, narration, title cards or edit from v1.

## Visual direction

A green-and-white studio scene replaces the earlier flat dark direction, following user feedback. A real emerald 3D P has rounded extrusion, a raised white pulse ribbon and cast shadows. Protocol pages, rings and particles orbit as the camera moves. Bahnschrift display typography and Segoe UI supporting type carry the white, emerald and sage identity throughout. No stock medical footage or generated product UI.

## Story

| Approximate time | Direction |
|---|---|
| 0–6.7 | Moving 3D mark and protocol pages on a white studio background: “From protocol. To progress.” Motion begins from frame one. |
| 6.7–17.6 | Reveal the real app; cursor uploads the fictional protocol, triggers actual extraction, opens its source clause, switches to Clinical Lead and approves. |
| 17.6–28.3 | Click into screening, start the cohort run, inspect three outcomes, open P-083 uncertainty, then return to the cohort and P-042. |
| 28.3–30.8 | Confirm external participation. |
| 30.8–40.7 | Click Safety, submit the synthetic medication record and follow its finding into a case with genuine AI investigation. |
| 40.7–55 | Inspect source evidence, type a human rationale, cite the record and submit an acknowledgement. A smooth camera move follows the reviewer. |
| 55–60 | The pulse route resolves into the bespoke P logo: “Keep the trial moving. Bring tomorrow’s medicines closer.” |

The edit follows actual navigation and scroll movement from a single browser recording. Only genuine processing waits are compressed; speed changes are documented. Cursor and click halos are injected recording annotations, with no influence on application state. Forms use actual typing. Data, source content, results and app styling remain unmodified.

## Sound and budget

Final MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD narration includes inclusion/exclusion criteria, cohort wording without the patient count, time saved and potential patient impact. The rejected soundtrack was replaced by sparse ambient ElevenLabs Music v2.5, mixed quietly at -36 LUFS under -16 LUFS narration. Scribe V2 supplies word timing. Combined published-rate estimate is approximately USD 1.55 including superseded iterations; total new fal allowance is USD 10. All 3D rendering is local. A separate fresh capture database preserves the shared cohort and history.

## Clinical accuracy

AI proposes and investigates; reviewed backend rules compute outcomes. People approve rules, confirm participation and record clinical decisions. High priority describes review priority. Monitoring is an in-app case notification, not an external hospital alert. The wider benefit is potential operational efficiency, not a guarantee of earlier regulatory approval.
